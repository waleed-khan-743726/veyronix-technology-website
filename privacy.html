Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026. Veyronix Technology Private Limited ("Veyronix", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when submitting a project inquiry, scheduling a consultation, or contacting us directly via email or phone. This may include your name, business email address, phone number, company name, website, and project scope requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • • Evaluate your technical requirements and respond to your project brief.
  • • Provide transactional email receipts and scheduling links.
  • • Maintain our internal operational lead records and communication history.
  • • Comply with applicable legal and business obligations.

3. Data Storage & Security

Inbound project briefs are securely processed through our serverless API and stored in enterprise Google Cloud infrastructure. We employ industry-standard encryption, strict access control, and automated formula injection sanitization to protect your information against unauthorized access.

4. Analytics & Tracking

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregated website performance, device categories, and general traffic sources. We do not use invasive fingerprinting, keystroke logging, or sell your personal data to third parties.
