WORKFLOW ORCHESTRATION

1. AI Business Automation

The Business Problem:

Teams waste countless hours copying data between forms, inboxes, CRMs, and spreadsheets. When steps are manual, handoffs get missed, reports are delayed, and operational bottlenecks strangle business growth.

What Veyronix Builds:

We design event-driven multi-step automation pipelines using n8n, Make, and custom Python microservices. We build self-healing workflows with automated retries, error logging, and Slack/email notifications.

Typical Systems Connected
n8n · Make · Zapier · Webhooks · Google Workspace · Slack · Airtable · PostgreSQL
Business Result

Zero manual data entry, instant operational handoffs, and consistent error-free execution across your software stack.

2. AI Agents & Conversational Assistants

The Business Problem:

Basic rule-based chatbots frustrate users with rigid menus and inability to resolve actual issues. Meanwhile, raw LLMs hallucinate unless bounded by strict architectural guardrails.

What Veyronix Builds:

Capabilities & Stack
OpenAI API · Anthropic Claude · Tool-Calling · Vector DBs · RAG · FastAPI
Business Result

Automated 24/7 lead qualification, accurate customer support resolutions, and real-time internal operational search.
