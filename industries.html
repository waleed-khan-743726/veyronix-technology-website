HEALTHCARE & MEDICAL PRACTICES

Healthcare, Wellness & Dental Clinics

Front-desk staff at clinics spend significant portions of their day manually answering repetitive questions, scheduling appointments, and sending confirmation reminders.

Systems We Can Build
  • • Automated 24/7 patient inquiry & intake workflows
  • • Two-way SMS appointment confirmation & rescheduling
  • • Post-consultation review & feedback capture
  • • Secure intake form data transfer to practice CRM
Operational Outcome

Significant reduction in no-show rates, faster patient response times outside office hours, and more time for staff to focus on in-clinic patient care.

REAL ESTATE & PROPERTY

Real Estate Agencies & Property Developers

Property inquiries arrive from multiple portals and social ad campaigns. Speed-to-lead is critical; waiting an hour often means losing the buyer to a competing agency.

Systems We Can Build
  • • Instant multi-channel lead capture into GoHighLevel
  • • Automated qualification scoring (budget, area, timeline)
  • • Dynamic viewing appointment scheduling with agents
  • • Long-term automated property alert sequences via SMS/Email
Operational Outcome

Sub-60-second first response to new property inquiries and zero unassigned leads, maximizing ROI on ad spend.
